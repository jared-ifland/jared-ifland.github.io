Before coming to Davis, I received my M.A. in Philosophy and my B.A. in Philosophy and Mathematics, both from Florida State University. Before that, I grew up on the "Other West Coast" in Tampa, Florida, which (fun fact) is considered by many to be the birthplace of death metal.

Outside of philosophy, I enjoy traveling, writing and recording music, and photography. All images featured on this site (excluding those which include myself) are ones that I have taken over the years and are often the result of my travels.
